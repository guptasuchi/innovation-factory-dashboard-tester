The Innovation Factory turns NAAMII's research into companies. It is being set up right now, and we are looking for three kinds of people. Pick the one that sounds like you.

Help build the Factory before it exists.

NAAMII has spun out three deep-tech companies from its research since 2019: Tangible Careers, NHU.AI and DIYO.AI. The Innovation Factory is how we make that structured and repeatable.

We're inviting founding advisors before the hard questions are settled. We want people who will argue with us now, not admire the result later.

Where your time goes

A founding advisor gives time and expertise in two places. Operations comes first; startups come later, and only if you want to.

What we're asking of you

A sharp answer on one question is worth more than polite interest in all of them.

Engage with the questionsThe two or three where you have scar tissue.
Anchor one areaLegal structure, capital, programme design, IP, or global go-to-market.
Open a doorA lawyer who's done this, a DFI contact, an operator worth copying.

Milestones for building the initiative itself.

10 to 15 founding advisors on board. Entity registered, governance and investment committee in place. Programme design revised against real advice. Selection machinery built and ready.
